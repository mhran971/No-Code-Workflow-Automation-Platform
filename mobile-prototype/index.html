Sales Procedures Playbook

This playbook defines the approved procedures for the sales team, ensuring consistency in client interactions and deal closure processes.

Chapter 1: Sales Cycle Stages

  • Initial prospect qualification
  • Discovery call and requirements gathering
  • Proposal preparation and pricing
  • Contract review with legal team
  • Formal signing and deal closure
  • Handover to customer success

Chapter 2: Qualification Criteria

Verify budget availability, decision-maker authority, and project timeline before escalating to management for review and resource allocation.

Chapter 3: CRM Requirements

All deal stages must be logged in the CRM within 24 hours of the interaction. Missing entries will result in pipeline reporting gaps.
